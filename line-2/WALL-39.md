The full-resolution line-2 wall was absent. Fetching the public artifact from
record 38 failed with a name-resolution error; the web tool also could not
retrieve it. The retained `dist/line-2/38.jpg` was the only available line-2
ancestor. The installed image editor continued that preview, preserving the
recognizable framing, Pepe figures, rock, water, cracks and inherited motifs
while adding the lower-left balance-stone mark.

**Unmet preservation requirement:** this is a generated continuation from a
512-pixel JPEG, not a verified additive edit of the original full-resolution
PNG. Exact ancestral rock and mark detail cannot be recovered or certified;
generation can redraw small details. It must not be described as pixel-identical
outside an addition region. The original size is restored using the dimensions
recorded by the previous contributor, not measured from the unavailable PNG.

Chosen review: inspect the whole finished PNG and decoded JPEG against the
available ancestor preview. Central Pepe retains the broad mouth and heavy
lidded eyes; the cave remains warm and earthy, with visible water and familiar
landmarks. The new motif adds no hands or handprints. Existing hand details are
too indistinct to certify five digits, an inherited unresolved visual limitation.
No added text, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, frames, real
people or prohibited symbols were observed. Inherited pictograms remain.
The ancestral photographic-looking rock is retained in appearance.

The review, hashes, dimensions and limitations are recorded in
`wall-review-39.json`. Local visual review and byte checks are not independent
certification. No image requirement is silently claimed to have passed.
